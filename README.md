# Human Truth Driven Design (HTDD)

> *Technology has never been easier to build.*
>
> *Understanding people has never been more important.*

Artificial intelligence has fundamentally changed software development.

Ideas that once required years of engineering can now become working products in days. The ability to build software is rapidly becoming abundant.

As technology becomes easier to create, something else becomes far more valuable.

The future belongs to those who understand people before they build for them. Human Truth Driven Design is a methodology for discovering enduring human truths and translating them into experiences that technology can faithfully serve.

**Knowing what is worth building.**

A working map of the method, terms, and practices is in [00_METHOD_INDEX.md](00_METHOD_INDEX.md). What a public-beta reader may rely on is in [BETA.md](BETA.md).

Human Truth Driven Design (HTDD) is an open-source framework, philosophy, and methodology for creating products that begin with enduring truths about people before they begin with technology. The framework, methodology, and reusable starter kit in this repository are available under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).

As AI accelerates our ability to create, HTDD helps ensure we create things that genuinely improve people's lives.

---

# Why HTDD Exists

Most product methodologies begin after the problem has already been defined.

They ask questions like:

- What problem are we solving?
- Who is the user?
- What features should we build?
- How should the interface work?

These are important questions.

They simply are not the first questions.

HTDD begins one step earlier.

Before we design solutions, we seek understanding.

Before we define features, we seek truth.

Before we optimize experiences, we ask whether we truly understand the human experience itself.

Technology changes.

Human beings do not.

That is where HTDD begins.

---

# Our Mission

To help founders, designers, engineers, researchers, and organizations build products that improve people's lives by first understanding the enduring truths that shape human experience.

We believe technology should adapt to humanity, not require humanity to adapt to technology.

Every project begins by asking: **What human relationship are we trying to improve?**

For Forever After Memories, that relationship is between people and loved ones who have died. Other possible applications include patients' relationship with uncertainty, students' relationship with learning, and people's relationship with financial confidence. These are directions for inquiry, not additional completed case studies.

---

# The Core Belief

Every meaningful product should be traceable back to an enduring Human Truth.

If a feature cannot explain which Human Truth it serves, it deserves to be questioned.

More importantly, every implementation should preserve the original human intent that inspired it.

HTDD is designed to preserve that intent across every stage of product creation.

As products grow, teams expand, and technology evolves, the original understanding of people should become clearer—not weaker.

Human Truths become Product Principles.

Product Principles become Experience Principles.

Experience Principles create Experience Rhythm.

Experience Rhythm produces Experience Boards.

Experience Boards guide Interaction Design.

Interaction Design shapes Interfaces.

Interfaces become Technology.

Technology is where understanding becomes tangible.

---

# What is a Human Truth?

A Human Truth is a discovery about what it means to be human that remains true regardless of technology, culture, or time.

Human Truths are not:

- User needs
- Pain points
- Requirements
- Features
- Market opportunities

Those things evolve.

Human Truths endure.

Examples include:

- People need to be received before they are invited to participate.
- People reconnect emotionally before they process information.
- Recognition is not identification. Recognition is reunion.

Human Truths are not invented.

They are discovered.

Human Truths explain why.

# The HTDD Lifecycle

Every project built using Human Truth Driven Design follows the same progression.

```text
Founder Experience
        ↓
Human Truth Discovery
        ↓
Truth Validation
        ↓
Product Principles
        ↓
Experience Principles
        ↓
Experience Rhythm
        ↓
Experience Boards
        ↓
Interaction Principles
        ↓
Interface Design
        ↓
Prototype
        ↓
Human Validation
        ↓
Engineering
```

Notice where technology appears.

Near the end.

Not the beginning.

# Experience Boards

Experience Boards are the bridge between human understanding and product design.

Rather than describing screens or features, they describe the emotional responsibility of each meaningful moment in an experience.

Every Experience Board asks:

- What human experience are we protecting?
- What should someone feel?
- What should the product intentionally refuse to do?
- What designed object replaces the ordinary product move?
- How should AI behave?
- What must engineering preserve?
- How do we know the experience succeeded?

A refusal is not an empty screen. The board must name the ordinary product move being refused and the positive form that holds the moment.

Experience Boards ensure implementation remains faithful to the Human Truths from which they were derived.

How to write them: [METHODOLOGY/EXPERIENCE_BOARDS.md](METHODOLOGY/EXPERIENCE_BOARDS.md).

Experience Architecture explains how that truth unfolds through time.

# Designing With Restraint

Human Truth Driven Design is not only concerned with what should exist.

It is equally concerned with what should intentionally remain absent.

Sometimes the highest expression of design is restraint.

Silence.

Stillness.

Waiting.

Space.

Permission.

Reflection.

Meaningful experiences are shaped as much by what is withheld as by what is presented.

Restraint still needs a designed form. An empty screen reads as unfinished work, and unfinished work invites the ordinary product move back in.

Copy that explains the product's restraint can keep the product central. Self-explaining virtue is still Product Gravity.

How to protect a build from that pull: [METHODOLOGY/PRODUCT_GRAVITY.md](METHODOLOGY/PRODUCT_GRAVITY.md).

# Design Boundaries

Human Truths tell us what should exist.

Design Boundaries protect what should never be compromised.

They define the ethical and experiential limits within which products are allowed to evolve.

Examples include:

- The visitor moves. The product waits.
- Presence is evoked, never simulated.
- AI is the steward of family-provided truth, never its source.
- Silence is a first-class element of the experience.
- The product leaves first.

Design Boundaries exist to preserve trust as products evolve.

# Why Founder Experience Matters

Many meaningful products begin because someone experiences something they cannot ignore.

A moment of loss.

A frustrating system.

An overlooked community.

A relationship that deserves better.

Founder Experience is not accepted as truth simply because it is personal.

Instead, it becomes the starting point for discovery.

The role of Human Truth is to determine whether that experience reflects something universal.

# Creativity in the Age of AI

Artificial intelligence can generate interfaces.

It can generate code.

It can generate ideas.

But it cannot independently determine what is worth building.

That remains a profoundly human responsibility.

HTDD treats creativity differently.

Creativity is not simply generating ideas.

Creativity is discovering truths that were always there but had not yet been articulated.

As AI becomes increasingly capable of generating solutions, HTDD becomes increasingly concerned with understanding when technology should remain intentionally restrained.

The role of AI is not to replace human relationships, creativity, or meaning.

Its role is to support them with humility.

When a Human Truth forbids invention, the implementation job is stricter than “make the content feel warm.” Define source, then only carry it. How that works in practice: [METHODOLOGY/ENTRUSTED_SOURCE.md](METHODOLOGY/ENTRUSTED_SOURCE.md).

The greatest advantage in the AI era will not belong to those who build the fastest.

It will belong to those who understand people the deepest.

# Case Studies

Human Truth Driven Design evolves through evidence.

Every principle in this repository is expected to be challenged, refined, or strengthened through real products built using the methodology.

## Forever After Memories

Forever After Memories is one worked example of Human Truth Driven Design. The [recognition board](CASE_STUDIES/forever-after-memories/STORYBOARD.md) is published as an illustration. It does not bind other products. Each product names its own exclusions.

Beginning with a deeply personal founder experience, it has helped shape the methodology through discoveries about remembrance, presence, recognition, reflection, AI, and human relationships.

The methodology did not create the case study.

The case study continues to refine the methodology. Other products do not inherit its implementation rules.

# Start a project

Copy `STARTER_KIT/` into the product repository. The product then owns those files. This repository stays the source of the methodology.

Recommended destination: `docs/htdd/`.

Also valid: `docs/`, `htdd/`, `design/htdd/`, or any path the team already uses for product writing.

1. Copy the folder to the destination.
2. Fill in `SOURCE.md` with the copy date and, optionally, a commit or tag from this repository.
3. Add one sentence to the product README so people and agents can find the work: "Human Truth work lives in `docs/htdd/`." Use the path you chose.
4. Start at `PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md`. Agents start at `AGENTS.md`. Do not copy the kit over a folder that already has project work. If the kit later changes, merge by hand. Copies that already have the kit follow [Existing product repositories](STARTER_KIT/README.md#existing-product-repositories).

Steps and file list: [STARTER_KIT/README.md](STARTER_KIT/README.md).

Workspace layout: [Project Structure](METHODOLOGY/PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md). Discovery prompts: [Human Truth Discovery Workbook](STARTER_KIT/HUMAN_TRUTH_DISCOVERY.md), a draft practice aid.

# The Goal

The purpose of Human Truth Driven Design is not to help teams build more software.

It is to help them build software that remains faithful to the people it was created to serve.

Every artifact in HTDD exists to answer one question:

> **Does this decision still honor the Human Truth that inspired it?**

If the answer is no, the methodology asks us to stop and understand why before moving forward.

---

# A Living Methodology

HTDD is not presented as complete.

No methodology understands people perfectly.

Human Truth Driven Design exists to help teams ask better questions, observe more carefully, and remain willing to change when people teach us something new.

Every project built using HTDD becomes evidence.

Every discovery strengthens—or challenges—the methodology itself.

The framework is intended to evolve through practice.

---

# The Promise

Human Truth Driven Design does not promise better products simply because a team follows a process.

It promises something different.

That every meaningful decision remains connected to the people the product exists to serve.

Products will evolve.

Technology will change.

Artificial intelligence will continue to reshape how software is created.

Human Truths endure.

By beginning with those truths, we believe we can build products that remain meaningful long after today's technology has become tomorrow's history.

---

# An Invitation

This repository is not intended to be the final word on product development.

It is an invitation.

To question assumptions.

To discover Human Truths.

To build with greater intention.

To remember that software is ultimately about people.

Ideas are easy to generate.

Wisdom is knowing which ideas deserve to exist.

Products do not lose their humanity all at once.

They lose it one decision at a time.

Human Truth Driven Design exists to ensure every decision—from the founder's first insight to the final implementation—can be traced back to the people the product was created to serve.

Technology will continue to evolve.

Artificial intelligence will become more capable.

Building software will become faster, cheaper, and more accessible than ever before.

But the questions that matter most will remain profoundly human.

What do people need?

What gives life meaning?

What helps us feel connected?

What makes an experience unforgettable?

The future will not belong to those who simply build more.

It will belong to those who understand more deeply.
