# Glossary

Shared language for Human Truth Driven Design.

These definitions are for readers and teams. They are not a complete philosophy. If a term is still being learned in practice, that is said plainly.

---

## Human Truth

A discovery about what it means to be human that remains true regardless of technology, culture, or time.

A Human Truth is not a user need, a pain point, a requirement, or a feature. Those things evolve. Human Truths endure. They explain why.

---

## Experience Gift

The human good a moment is responsible for giving. Safety. Reunion. Companionship. Peace. Permission.

A gift is not a feature and not a metric. It is the reason the moment exists.

---

## Experience Board

The specification of a moment's emotional responsibility. It names what someone should feel, what the product must refuse, what designed object replaces the ordinary product move, how AI should behave, and how the team will know the moment succeeded.

A board is not a screen inventory.

---

## Experience Arc

The whole emotional journey. Boards are moments. The arc is the coherence of those moments over time.

Success is measured by the whole, not by the perfection of any single board.

---

## Experience Rhythm

The way one beat becomes the next. Rhythm is temperature, pacing, and permission. It is not navigation.

---

## Product Gravity

The pull toward a familiar product pattern that is nearest to the moment being built.

Arrival becomes onboarding. Recognition becomes a profile page. Presence becomes content consumption. Reflection becomes an idle state. Continuation becomes retention.

Product Gravity is the primary implementation risk in HTDD. Naming the failure mode is often more useful than listing features.

Warmer copy is a Product Gravity check. A refusal or a replacement names the ordinary product move and the positive designed object. Reassurance (“that’s okay”) and a pointer at future contribution are ordinary product moves inside an empty moment the experience was meant to hold.

---

## Refusal pattern

What the product must not become in a given moment.

A refusal is incomplete until it also names the positive designed object that replaces the ordinary product move. A refusal is not an empty screen.

---

## Residue

What remains when the product withdraws. Residue can be atmosphere. It cannot be chrome, an empty-state widget, or a bordered box that reads as missing implementation.

---

## Progressive recession

The visible reduction of product language and chrome as the relationship becomes more central.

Each beat should justify every remaining word.

---

## Design Boundary

An ethical or experiential limit that must not be crossed as the product evolves.

Examples: the visitor moves and the product waits; presence is evoked, never simulated; the product leaves first.

---

## Readiness

The condition in which the next emotional moment can begin without force.

Time can stand in for readiness in a prototype. Time is not readiness. Observed, not doctrine.

---

## Review chrome

Interface added so a steward can inspect the work: state names, a scrollable map, debug labels.

Review chrome is a method tool. It must stay labeled as such, or the prototype starts teaching the visitor a procedure. Observed, not doctrine.

---

## Arc Steward

The person responsible for protecting Human Truths, Experience Gifts, refusal patterns, and Product Gravity failure modes during build and review.

Green tests do not replace Arc Steward playback.

Automated checks can establish behavior and guardrails. They are not approval by a named human reviewer, including the Arc Steward. A project names its own reviewers. Observed from practice.

---

## Entrusted source

Material the product is allowed to render, restore, organize, or compose from.

Representative content may be authored. It must be authored as a closed source kit, not as inferred personality. Observed, not a new Human Truth.

---

## Ranked artifact chain

The order of authority used when documents conflict during implementation.

Experience Boards describe hoped-for emotional responsibility. Later implementation artifacts may be stricter. If rank is not explicit, implementers will choose the warmer document. Observed, not doctrine.

---

## Completion class

The kind of finish a ticket is allowed to claim.

Specification. Implementation. Automated verification. Human validation. Freeze or handoff.

An implementation ticket closes when its implementation and automated acceptance criteria pass. Human validation the milestone has deferred stays on the validation ticket. Observed from practice, not doctrine.

---

## Skipped reproducer

A check the team could not run, kept as a governed finding.

It names its blocker, the owning ticket, the condition that removes the skip, and its current status. Observed from practice, not doctrine.

---

## Participant-testing gate

The check that a frozen build can be walked as the intended end-to-end product journey before participants are asked to use it.

A freeze of one portion, including a faithful emotional arc, does not pass this gate. This is not emotional readiness. Observed from practice, not doctrine.
