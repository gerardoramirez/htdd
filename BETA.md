# Public method beta

Status: scope of the public method. Updated: 2026-11-16.

This repository is the free methodology. It is a beta of the practice. It is not a finished registry of Human Truths, and it is not the HTDD product.

Copying the starter kit does not validate a truth. A project observation does not become a universal truth by being written down here.

## What you can follow

These pages are settled enough to guide a team:

- [README](README.md) for the philosophy
- [How to read HTDD](00_METHOD_INDEX.md) for the working map
- [Why HTDD Exists](FOUNDATIONS/WHY_HTDD_EXISTS.md) and the [Glossary](FOUNDATIONS/GLOSSARY.md)
- [Experience Boards](METHODOLOGY/EXPERIENCE_BOARDS.md), [Experience Arcs](METHODOLOGY/EXPERIENCE_ARCS.md), [Experience Rhythm](METHODOLOGY/EXPERIENCE_RHYTHM.md), [Product Gravity](METHODOLOGY/PRODUCT_GRAVITY.md), [Prototype Practice](METHODOLOGY/PROTOTYPE_PRACTICE.md), and [Entrusted Source](METHODOLOGY/ENTRUSTED_SOURCE.md)
- [Project Structure](METHODOLOGY/PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md) and the [starter kit](STARTER_KIT/README.md)

Start a product by copying `STARTER_KIT/`. On a new copy, a person starts at [INIT.md](STARTER_KIT/INIT.md) and answers there before either bootstrap. The product then owns those files. `MILESTONES/CURRENT.md` names no governing milestone until the project writes one. `001_EXAMPLE_MILESTONE` is a template. A first milestone names the exclusions that product binds. It does not inherit another product's list.

## One worked example

The [Forever After Memories recognition board](CASE_STUDIES/forever-after-memories/STORYBOARD.md) is one product's illustration, published with this method. It shows how an emotional responsibility becomes interface behavior. It does not supply exclusions, sentences, or implementation rules for any other product. No participant results are claimed there.

## Outside this beta

`htdd-core` is the separate product. Its application, accounts, and project records are not in this repository. `htdd-canon`, `htdd-eval`, and `htdd-state` are separate as well. This beta does not include a web app, Ask HTDD, billing, or a hosted review loop.

Questions about the method belong in this repository's issues. Participant evidence from a product stays outside Git.
