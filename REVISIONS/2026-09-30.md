# 2026-09-30

A product milestone kept emotional intent, safety boundaries, implementation, and validation connected across a long build. The same practice left several different finishes under one word for done.

Nothing here is a new Human Truth. Logged as [DISC-009](../DISCOVERIES/DISCOVERIES.md).

---

## What was accomplished

The lifecycle is now explicit in [Prototype Practice](../METHODOLOGY/PROTOTYPE_PRACTICE.md), the [Glossary](../FOUNDATIONS/GLOSSARY.md), and the starter-kit operating rules.

A ticket names one completion class: Specification, Implementation, Automated verification, Human validation, or Freeze or handoff. It closes inside that class. State is recorded separately: Implemented, Automatically verified, Ready for validation, Human validated, Accepted.

An implementation ticket closes when its implementation and automated acceptance criteria pass. Deferred human validation stays on the milestone validation ticket.

Each milestone summary carries one status record: dependency order, merged pull requests, open findings, deferred validation, the latest authoritative test counts, the freeze candidate, and the accepted freeze.

Simulator, device, assistive-technology, and named-reviewer work sit on that validation ticket unless one feature is unusually high risk.

Before a group of production surfaces is built, one governing contract specifies purpose, hierarchy, states, language, accessibility, and visual components. A second documentation ticket that restates it is the same work split in two.

A follow-up pull request states whether it completes a ticket, repairs a finding, adds evidence, or changes the governing contract.

A skipped reproducer is a finding. It names its blocker, owning ticket, removal condition, and status.

A participant-testing candidate can be walked as the intended end-to-end product journey. A freeze of one portion, including the emotional arc alone, is not that candidate.

Automated checks can establish behavior and guardrails. They are not approval by a named human reviewer, including the Arc Steward. That milestone's design, accessibility, and privacy reviewers stay examples of roles a project may name. They are not required titles for every product.

---

## What practice kept

The arc and the governing contract stay the behavioral reference for implementation.

Small numbered tickets stay. Implementation and evidence stay on separate branches. Participant evidence stays outside Git. Reviews record the exact commit and freeze identifier they used.

Concern, Blocker, Pass, skipped reproducer, and unresolved scope stay visible.

Named human review stays a human gate. On this milestone those seats included design review, accessibility review, and the Arc Steward. The method caught a real gap when emotional-arc validation was asked to stand in for a complete application journey. That distinction stays.

## What was deliberately not claimed

This is one milestone's operating friction. It is not validated on a second product. It is not a Human Truth.

The five classes and five states are the lifecycle that removed the confusion in this milestone. They are not a claim that every future product must invent more states.

Projects that already copied the kit do not receive this by refreshing `METHOD/`. Compare `AGENTS.md` and add the status-record section to the governing `MILESTONE_SUMMARY.md`. Do not overwrite filled milestone work.
