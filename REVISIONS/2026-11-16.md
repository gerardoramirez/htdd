## What was accomplished

The still-in-force rule is the same in the methodology and the starter kit. A first milestone names that product's exclusions. A later milestone restates what remains, or names the predecessor file. A sentence that only names the predecessor is not that list. Another product's list is not copied forward.

An empty moment the experience was meant to hold is the warmer-copy check. Reassurance and a pointer at future contribution are ordinary product moves there. The positive designed object still has to be named.

`001_EXAMPLE_MILESTONE` is a template. `MILESTONES/CURRENT.md` names no governing milestone until a project writes one.

[BETA.md](../BETA.md) states what a public-beta reader may follow, which pages are drafts, and that the product application is outside this repository. [NOTICE](../NOTICE) names the copyright holder. The recognition board stays as one product's illustration.
