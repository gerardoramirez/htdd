# 2026-10-03

A long build showed where a finishable path stays finishable. Later work can confirm an exclusion, repair a defect, or ask for evidence. It can also reopen the backlog by adding product so the app feels more finished.

Nothing here is a new Human Truth. Logged as [DISC-010](../DISCOVERIES/DISCOVERIES.md).

---

## What was accomplished

The observations are now explicit in [Product Gravity](../METHODOLOGY/PRODUCT_GRAVITY.md), [Prototype Practice](../METHODOLOGY/PROTOTYPE_PRACTICE.md), [Entrusted Source](../METHODOLOGY/ENTRUSTED_SOURCE.md), and the starter-kit operating rules.

A later phase that meets an excluded move again withholds or retires it. Confirming the exclusion leaves the earlier design in force. The old surface does not become the next brief.

A defect stays inside the current shell. A crash, an overflow, or a noisy log is repaired inside the governing milestone and the current beat.

A ticket names what it will not absorb, including the next product slice. A quality pass restates the milestone's job. Accessibility, device playback, and named review can still be open after the code has merged.

Whether work is done is read from the milestone status record and the merged pull requests. A chat or a session capture is not the lasting record.

Engineering review may improve a build before participants see it. It stays labeled as non-participant review.

When presentation lowers the person, fix the carrying language. Do not answer it by inventing a richer account.

---

## What practice kept

Refusals still name a positive designed object. Ranked governance may still be stricter than a warmer board. Green tests still prove absence. Merged, verified, and accepted stay different events. Participants still need the whole journey.

The product story that taught these rules stays in that product. These pages keep the operating pattern only.
