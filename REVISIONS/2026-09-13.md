## What was accomplished

A visitor to the repository can start at [00_METHOD_INDEX.md](../00_METHOD_INDEX.md), look up terms in the [Glossary](../FOUNDATIONS/GLOSSARY.md), and follow essays for boards, arcs, rhythm, Product Gravity, prototype practice, and entrusted source.

That product kept the detailed implementation lessons in its own learning artifacts. The product `METHOD/` snapshot was not overwritten.

## Reflection

Named Product Gravity is an implementation tool. The failure mode “Reflection becomes an idle state” made ordinary product moves visibly wrong before build.

Instructional restraint is still Product Gravity. Copy that explains withdrawal keeps the product central.

A refusal is not an empty screen. Residue can be atmosphere; it cannot be chrome.

As the relationship becomes more central, product language and chrome should recede.

Review questions may be used during build, not only at final QA.

Logged as [DISC-006](../DISCOVERIES/DISCOVERIES.md).

## Continuation and transitions

Observed, not validation of Continuation or the six transitions.

HTDD is strong at telling a team what not to build. It is still less complete at telling a prototype when a moment is ready.

An Experience Gift that occurs outside the product still needs a governed threshold.

Refusal patterns implement faster than positive gifts. They do not prove Permission or release.

Time can proxy readiness. If the fallback cannot be lingered in, it feels like a funnel.

Review chrome is a method tool. Green tests prove absence. They do not replace Arc Steward playback.

Edge transitions need their own ticket shape.

Open gap: how to test Arrival from a CTA without letting the CTA contaminate the arc.

Logged as [DISC-007](../DISCOVERIES/DISCOVERIES.md).

## Representative content and AI narration

Observed, not a new Human Truth.

HTDD changed what implementation was allowed to build. The job became: define source, then only carry it.

Representative content must be authored as a closed source kit, not as inferred personality.

Narration may restore, read, organize, or compose from that kit. It must not become a plausible story about the person.

Later governance can be stricter than an Experience Board. If rank is not explicit, implementers choose the warmer document. That is evidence HTDD needs a ranked artifact chain.

The five-question AI Narration Transition Test survived contact with implementation.

When a governance trigger calls for a DDR and the DDR does not exist, proceed only if the ranked chain gives enough authority, and record the missing DDR as an explicit gap.

Open question: is “optional narration” a visitor control, or can restraint mean the narration has already finished?

Shareable sentence:

> Representative content became implementable when the team defined a closed source kit and refused to speak beyond it.

Logged as [DISC-008](../DISCOVERIES/DISCOVERIES.md). Guide: [Entrusted Source](../METHODOLOGY/ENTRUSTED_SOURCE.md).

## Operating-system decisions

`AGENTS.md` must inspect the latest approved milestone. Incoming artifacts belong in that milestone's `implementation_artifacts/` folder. Practice exposed a stale pointer to a different milestone; the portable lesson is to resolve the governing milestone explicitly rather than hardcode a folder name.

If `PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md` is incomplete and the milestone implementation chain is complete, implementation may proceed from the chain. Do not invent founder meaning.

When code comments and markdown governance drift, markdown wins.

`METHOD/` stays a reserved snapshot. Project `HUMAN_TRUTHS/` is not the canonical HTDD registry.
