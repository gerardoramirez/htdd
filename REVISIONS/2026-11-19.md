# 2026-11-19

A product that already exists can take the kit without the running app becoming the bootstrap. A team can name more than two people, and a milestone can record a decision to do something later.

Nothing here is a new Human Truth. Nothing here is a case study.

---

## What was accomplished

The internal bootstrap now asks for three kinds of people when they exist: direct users, internal users, and the person at the far end. The owning team is not automatically a user. An experience that was not lived is written as an inference. Each person gets one line on how the product serves them, and what they must never reach. That boundary is recorded on the bootstrap. It binds when a person writes it into the still-in-force list. One sentence is a complete answer on Success. The discovery workbook asks for the same three kinds.

The example milestone has a Deferred section: what waits, why it waits, and what would make it current. The prompt that asked what sits outside this milestone moved there. The two refusal questions stay with the still-in-force list. An agent must not build a deferred item in the governing milestone, move it into that list, or close it as answered. Deferred validation in the status record is a different line.

[When the product already exists](../STARTER_KIT/README.md#when-the-product-already-exists) says how a person may label what is already in the repository. Baseline is the product that runs. Evidence is something a person lived or the team observed. Governing is only what a milestone names. Why and guardrails live in the kit. What the product does and how it is built stay in the product's own specs. If a spec disagrees with an exclusion or a principle, stop and ask a person. Classifying old documents is not a gate.

[SOURCE.md](../STARTER_KIT/SOURCE.md) now says where the optional commit hash comes from, and that the product README sentence naming this path is the only repository-root line the kit expects.

## What practice kept

A first milestone still names the exclusions that product binds. Another product's list is still not copied forward. An `[open]` line stays open until a person answers it. A missing `PROJECT.yaml` is still not `experience_arc: false`. The founder bootstrap is unchanged. The registry is still empty.

## What was deliberately not claimed

This does not validate a Human Truth. It does not publish a case study. It does not name one product's spec format, and it does not say that every spec yields. Existing code does not answer the bootstrap. A deferral is not an exclusion.

## Existing product repositories

Projects that already copied the kit do not receive this by refreshing `METHOD/`. They may add the empty Deferred heading, and the extra audience lines, by hand. Do not overwrite filled work. Do not invent an audience, a boundary, or a deferral.
