# Ethical AI Principles

What this product's AI must honor, and what it must refuse.

These are this project's principles. They are not method essays. They are not Experience Boards.

A later milestone inherits this file. Do not copy it into a milestone folder.

AI principles become usable when they can be translated into deterministic review checks. If a Human Truth forbids invention, narration may carry entrusted source. It must not become a plausible story about a person.
