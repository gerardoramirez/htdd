# Init

Begin here. This page is the whole of the first sitting.

Three questions. A person answers each one. If an agent is here, the agent may write the answer into [PROJECT.yaml](PROJECT.yaml). The agent does not choose, and does not suggest which answer sounds more serious.

Leave a value as `unset` until it has been answered. While any value is still `unset`, stay on this page. The bootstrap, the discovery workbook, and the milestones can wait.

Both answers to a question are a real way to begin. Neither one makes the product more worthy.

---

## 1. How do you meet the building?

This question is about you, the person who will sit with this work and with any agent beside you. It is not about the person who will use the product, and it is not about someone farther along who may never open it.

Some people have shipped software and can take the later words as they are. Some people are finding their way, with or without an agent helping. Either way, you can use this kit. Your answer does not open or close a milestone. It is kept so that later guidance can meet you where you are. Today it does not create a second set of rules.

- I am a skilled builder. Write `builder: skilled_builder`.
- I am learning the ropes. Write `builder: learning_the_ropes`.

## 2. Who is this work held by?

Some products begin with one person who cannot let the problem go. Others are held by a team. The team may sit inside a company. The product may serve people at another company, or someone who will never sign in.

We ask so the next page can speak to that holder, and so that no one invents a founder who is not there.

- A founder, or one person carrying it. Write `bootstrap: founder`. The next page is [PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md](PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md).
- A team, including an internal product or a product another company uses. Write `bootstrap: internal_b2b`. The next page is [PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP_INTERNAL.md](PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP_INTERNAL.md).

Leave the other bootstrap as the blank template. Do not copy answers from one into the other.

## 3. When you first build something, what should it be?

Some first versions are a journey a person feels: moments, a rhythm, an experience you would want to protect. Others are a tool or a repeated workflow, such as a portal, that helps someone get something done.

A tool can grow an experience later. This answer only sets the first milestone.

- An experience I want people to feel. Write `experience_arc: true`. Once a milestone governs, the arc files bind: the state map, the transition spec, the guardrail checklist, and the review script, along with beats, gifts, and an Arc Steward when the work needs one.
- A tool or workflow for getting something done. Write `experience_arc: false`. The first milestone can govern without those files. Do not invent an arc to fill the folder. The exclusions, the milestone summary, and, when you are ready to build, a governance note, a build brief, and tickets still belong to the work.

Open only the bootstrap named above.

Then [HUMAN_TRUTH_DISCOVERY.md](HUMAN_TRUTH_DISCOVERY.md).

If you are learning the ropes, these three answers are enough for today. The rest of the kit can wait until you reach it.
