# Human Truth Discovery Workbook

Status: draft practice aid. These prompts help a team distinguish a problem, an observation, a candidate Human Truth, and a Product Principle. Completing the workbook does not validate a truth.

Start from the bootstrap [INIT.md](INIT.md) names. When `PROJECT.yaml` sets `bootstrap` to `founder`, that page is [PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md](PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md). When it sets `internal_b2b`, that page is [PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP_INTERNAL.md](PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP_INTERNAL.md). Record what people taught you before proposing what to build. Keep identifiable participant evidence outside Git; use references to appropriately protected records.

Mark any line you cannot answer yet with `[open]`. Leave it open. An agent must not close an `[open]` by writing an answer.

## 1. Problem

What situation prompted the work? Who experiences it, and in what context?

When `bootstrap` is `internal_b2b`, name the direct user and the person at the far end. The person at the far end may never open the product. If they are the same person, say so.

## 3. Candidate Human Truth

What might this reveal about people beyond this particular product or technology? Write one sentence. Label it as a candidate, not an established truth.

## 4. Challenge the Candidate

- Could this instead be a need, preference, requirement, or feature idea?
- What evidence would contradict it?
- Whose experience is missing from the evidence?
- Where might culture, circumstance, or time change it?
- What further human review or research is needed?

Write here:

## 5. Product Principle

If the candidate holds, what responsibility follows for this product? Name both what the product should do and what it should refuse. Explain the connection to the candidate and its evidence.

Write here:

## 6. Experience and Decision

What moment would express that principle? What positive designed object replaces an ordinary product move you refuse? What observable signal would support or challenge the design?

When `bootstrap` is `internal_b2b`, the moment may be a task the direct user performs for a person who never opens the product. Name both people. When `experience_arc` is `false`, describe the operational moment. An emotional arc is not required on this page.

Record the decision, alternatives, uncertainty, and references to the evidence, candidate truth, principle, and eventual Experience Board.

Write here:

## 7. Next Review

Record a reviewer, the next evidence needed, and the current status: candidate, needs more evidence, revised, or rejected. A team may record its own review outcome without claiming universal validation.

When ready for the project's registry, use [HUMAN_TRUTHS/TEMPLATE.md](HUMAN_TRUTHS/TEMPLATE.md). Keep open questions attached. A project entry does not become a canonical HTDD truth automatically.
