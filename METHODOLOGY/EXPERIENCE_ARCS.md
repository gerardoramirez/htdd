# Experience Arcs

An Experience Arc is the whole emotional journey.

Boards protect moments. The arc protects the relationship between those moments.

A product can get every board right and still fail if the journey has no beginning, no middle, and no release.

---

## What an arc should hold

- Every arc should have a beginning, a middle, and a release.
- Every board should prepare the next one.
- Emotional gifts should accumulate rather than repeat.
- The product's role may change across the arc.
- Success is measured by the coherence of the whole, not the perfection of any single board.

---

## The product may become less central

Candidate principle, not yet a universal law:

> The best products become progressively less visible as the human experience becomes progressively more important.

This is not the same as: every product should disappear.

Products centered around relationships, remembrance, education, healing, creativity, or contemplation often should recede. Other products may need to remain visible. HTDD asks the team to know which kind of product they are building.

A second arc often appears in those first products:

```text
Product -> Person -> Relationship -> Life
```

---

## Progressive recession is how that arc becomes buildable

As the relationship becomes more central, product language and chrome should recede.

This can be a layout rule, not only a philosophy:

```text
Arrival: the product still speaks
Recognition: fewer words, the person appears
Presence: material-led, less labeling
Reflection: atmosphere, no title
Continuation: the least product language of all
```

Each beat should justify every remaining word.

---

## A gift outside the product still needs a threshold

Some Experience Gifts occur after the product has done its work. Permission to leave. A relationship that continues in life.

The product cannot design that gift itself. It can only design the door into it.

Without a governed threshold, the last screen becomes a completion page, a recap, a success state, or a retention opportunity.

Observed from practice, not doctrine:

> An Experience Gift that occurs outside the product still needs a governed threshold, or the last screen will become a completion page.

---

## Do not overclaim the arc

Translating an arc into a playable prototype is not the same as proving the gifts held.

Whether leaving feels respectful, whether stillness feels like peace, whether the product became less central — those remain review questions. They are not closed by implementation.
