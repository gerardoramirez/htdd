# Product Gravity

Product Gravity is the pull toward the familiar product pattern nearest to the moment being built.

The largest implementation risk in HTDD is not that an experience will be too quiet. It is that ordinary product instinct will replace the moment before anyone notices.

---

## Name the failure mode

The most useful build guidance is often not the feature list. It is a named failure mode.

Arrival becomes onboarding.
Recognition becomes a profile page.
Presence becomes content consumption.
Reflection becomes an idle state.
Continuation becomes retention.

Once the failure mode has a name, ordinary moves become visibly wrong: journals, continue buttons, welcome-back copy, how-do-you-feel prompts, recap screens, empty-state widgets.

A moment is not implementation-ready until its Product Gravity failure mode is named.

---

## Instructional restraint is still Product Gravity

Copy that explains restraint can keep the product central.

“The interface withdraws.”
“There is no response to give.”

These may sound aligned with the philosophy. They make the product narrate its own virtue.

HTDD treats self-explaining restraint as a Product Gravity risk.

---

## A refusal needs a designed object

Refusal patterns implement faster than positive gifts.

“Do not retain” is buildable early. “Make the visitor feel released” is not proven by the absence of a button.

The refusal must still point to a positive form. Residue can be atmosphere. It cannot be chrome.

If the designed object is missing, the refusal will be implemented as an empty screen, and the empty screen will later be filled by Product Gravity.

---

## Warmer copy is a Product Gravity check

Warmer copy is a Product Gravity check.

A refusal or a replacement names the ordinary product move and the positive designed object.

Reassurance (“that’s okay”) and a pointer at future contribution are ordinary product moves inside an empty moment the experience was meant to hold.

---

## A later phase confirms the exclusion

A quality-pass milestone cannot add a surface, collection, access path, invitation channel, or discovery query. If the work needs one, stop. Open a new milestone first. This is an implementation stop.

Observed from practice, not doctrine.

A later milestone may meet an excluded move again: an old tab, a prompt, a public surface. Withhold it, or retire it. Confirming the exclusion leaves the earlier design in force. The old surface does not become the brief for the next milestone.

A defect stays inside the current shell. A crash, an overflow, or a noisy log is repaired inside the governing milestone and the current beat. It does not become a new surface, a new product, or a new beat.

---

## Progressive recession resists gravity

As the relationship becomes more central, product language and chrome should recede.

Each beat should justify every remaining word.

This is one concrete way to keep Product Gravity from adding “just one more” label, title, or explanation.
