# Entrusted Source

Observed methodology learning. Not a new Human Truth.

When a Human Truth forbids invention, content and AI work are not invitations to make a believable person. They are a stricter job:

> Define source, then only carry it.

---

## Representative content is a closed source kit

If the product may not invent, representative content cannot mean “invent a plausible life.”

It must mean:

```text
Create a closed source kit.
Only render, organize, restore, or compose from that kit.
Do not infer personality, emotion, memory, or grief.
```

Representative content may be authored. It must be authored as entrusted source, not as inferred personality.

---

## Narration can only carry source

Narration should not create warmth by pretending to know the person.

Spoken language should be source text or minimal carrying language. The voice must not say “I am,” perform the person, address the visitor’s grief, or add emotional meaning the source did not provide.

> Narration can restore, read, organize, or compose from entrusted artifacts. It must not become a plausible story about the person.

When presentation lowers the person, fix the carrying language. Do not answer it by inventing a richer account.

---

## Principles become usable as checks

AI principles become usable when they can be translated into deterministic review checks.

```text
Is every spoken word source text or minimal carrying language?
Does the voice avoid “I am”?
Does it avoid performing the person?
Does it avoid addressing the visitor’s grief?
Can a family member identify the source material?
```

If a moment fails one question, it does not enter the prototype.

---

## Later governance may be stricter than a board

Experience Boards can describe hoped-for warmth that later governance refuses.

If authority is not explicit at implementation time, agents and engineers will choose the warmer document.

Implementation should follow the ranked chain when it exists. The chain operationalizes the boards. It may refuse warmth the board still hopes for.

This is evidence that HTDD needs a ranked artifact chain. Observed, not doctrine.

---

## Optional narration is still an open question

Silence can be first-class while narration is described as optional.

That leaves a method question:

> Is “optional narration” a visitor control, or can restraint mean the narration has already finished before the visitor is asked to interact?

Do not close this in implementation notes. Review it before a prototype is treated as emotionally complete.

---

## What this page does not claim

It does not claim a new Human Truth.

It records that existing HTDD governance can be sharp enough to stop a feed, a biography, a performed voice, invented personality, and grief interpretation inside a real build.
